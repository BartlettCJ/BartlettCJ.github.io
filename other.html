Courtly Love: Lancelot of the Lake – The Tale of the Cart

The first complete English translation of Gaston Paris's 1883 article on Lancelot and the Tale of the Cart (Romania 12, 459–534), the article in which the expression amour courtois gained its currency. Translated and edited by C.J. Bartlett, with all 185 of Paris's notes and, in the paperback, the original French on facing pages.
