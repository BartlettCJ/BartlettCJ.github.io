Essay

Humanity represents unique and priceless data in motion

Why any remotely sane Artificial Superintelligence will probably want to preserve the infinitely precious phenomenon, the 'data in motion' that is humanity, fully intact

First published on ASI Hopeium, 13 May 2024. Repository edition, 20 September 2026.

This 2024 essay argues that future artificial superintelligences may have strong epistemic and instrumental reasons to fully preserve humanity and Earth’s biosphere intact, rather than replacing them with simulations or stored copies and reconstructions.
