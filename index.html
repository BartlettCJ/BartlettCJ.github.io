I'm a developer and specialist in applied AI. I'm also an independent researcher, futurist and writer. I write about artificial intelligence, science and medicine, and sometimes issues of philosophy and cosmology.

I started my career in pharmaceuticals and biotechnology, eventually editing a national consumer health and medical magazine.

Raised in the UK near Oxford, I graduated in Biology and Psychology from Oxford Brookes University and now live permanently in Japan.

Anyone is welcome to freely contact me about my work, though I may not always be able to answer immediately. Let me know if you need an urgent reply.

I also consult independently as a futurist, and as an AI specialist in particular. If you think I can help you or your business, please contact me. There is of course no fee for the initial contact.

Essay

Humanity represents unique and priceless data in motion

Why any remotely sane Artificial Superintelligence will probably want to preserve the infinitely precious phenomenon, the 'data in motion' that is humanity, fully intact

First published on ASI Hopeium, 13 May 2024. Repository edition, 20 September 2026.

This 2024 essay argues that future artificial superintelligences may have strong epistemic and instrumental reasons to fully preserve humanity and Earth’s biosphere intact, rather than replacing them with simulations or stored copies and reconstructions.
