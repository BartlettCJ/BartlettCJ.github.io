Zero Without Stasis: Organisational Emergence in an Exactly Reversible Signed-Charge-Conserving Block Cellular Automaton

Working paper, version 1.49, 13 October 2026.

We found a simple artificial world in which random material reorganises into large, irregular regions, some rich in material and some nearly empty, while the net signed charge stays exactly zero and every microscopic step can be undone.

One Ring to Host It All: Fixed-Capacity Circular Realizations of Finite Functional Trajectories

Working paper, version 1.3, 29 October 2026.

Can a finite system that repeats on a single cycle reproduce, for a specified duration, the behaviour of another deterministic system, even when the visible behaviour appears irreversible? We formulate this as a circular-sequence problem.
